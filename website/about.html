Our story · Kukatpally & Moosapet, Hyderabad

Meet the dental specialists behind Care32, Kukatpally.

Care32 was built on a simple idea: dentistry shouldn't be something you dread. Meet the senior specialists, the philosophy and the technology behind the calmest dental clinic in Kukatpally and Moosapet.

Why we exist

Dentistry without the dread.

Most people don't avoid the dentist because of the cost. They avoid it because of fear - of pain, of long uncomfortable procedures, of not understanding what's being done to them. We started Care32 in Kukatpally to take all three of those fears off the table. Every treatment here begins with an unhurried conversation, continues with gentle, painless technique, and ends with you knowing exactly what was done and why.

"Super speciality" isn't just a name. In many clinics, one dentist handles everything. At Care32, each treatment is handled by a specialist in that field - endodontics, pediatric dentistry, orthodontics and more - under one roof, supported by visiting consultants for complex cases. You get the depth of a speciality hospital with the warmth of a neighbourhood clinic.

That's why families from Kukatpally, Moosapet, Greenhills Road, Rainbow Vistas Road, Anjaneya Nagar, Balanagar, KPHB and Borabanda keep coming back - and why our Google reviews talk less about teeth and more about how comfortable they felt in the chair.

Meet the doctors

Specialists you'll actually look forward to seeing.

A senior team with 20+ years of expertise across endodontics, restorative and cosmetic dentistry, supported by visiting consultants - known for patient, unhurried care.

Dr. Sandeep Nalla

MDS · Endodontics & Restorative Dentistry · 20+ years

Dr. Sandeep leads the endodontic and restorative side of Care32 - the root canals, crowns and rebuilds that save a natural tooth instead of removing it. After more than twenty years in practice, his work is defined less by speed than by precision: rotary endodontics, digital radiography and magnification let him find and clean canals that are easy to miss, which is why so much of his caseload is teeth other clinics had already given up on.

He is also the person patients are sent to when a previous root canal has failed. Re-treatment is slower, fiddlier work, and he takes it on routinely. Patients on Google mention the same three things every time: his expertise, the complete absence of pain, and the fact that he explains each step before he starts rather than after.

Specialities

  • Root canal re-treatment (failed or reinfected canals)
  • Post and core build-ups for badly broken teeth
  • Crowns, bridges and restorative rebuilds
  • Tooth-coloured fillings and cosmetic restorations
  • Digital imaging and magnified endodontics

Dr. Samatha Goparaju

BDS · Cosmetic & Family Dentistry · 20 years

Dr. Samatha handles the cosmetic and family side of the practice, from whitening and veneers through to full smile design. Twenty years in, her approach to cosmetic work is deliberately conservative: keep as much natural tooth as possible, match the result to the patient's own face rather than a template, and show them a preview before anything is prepared. Patients who arrive asking for a dramatic change often leave with something subtler and better suited to them.

Her other speciality is one that does not fit neatly on a certificate: she is remarkably good with frightened people. Families across Kukatpally and Moosapet bring their children to her for first check-ups, fluoride and cavity care, and reviewers repeatedly describe kids who arrived terrified and left asking when they can come back. Nervous adults get exactly the same unhurried treatment.

Our philosophy

How every visit works.

01

Listen first

Every treatment starts with an unhurried consultation. We hear your concern fully, examine carefully, and explain what we find in plain language - no jargon, no rushing.

02

Image & plan digitally

Digital radiography, intraoral cameras and 3D scanning let you see exactly what we see - so the treatment plan is something we agree on together, not something done to you.

03

Treat painlessly

Our zero-pain philosophy runs through everything: gentle anaesthesia, rotary endodontics and laser dentistry mean even the most feared treatments feel uneventful.

04

Protect long-term

We'd rather prevent the next problem than treat it. Clear aftercare, preventive advice and routine follow-ups keep your smile healthy between visits.

Technology in the clinic

State-of-the-art, so you barely notice.

The best dental technology is the kind that makes your visit shorter, gentler and more precise. Here's what works behind the scenes at Care32.

  • Digital imaging & radiography - sharper diagnosis with far lower radiation than film X-rays
  • Rotary endodontics - faster, gentler, single-visit root canals
  • Intraoral cameras - you see your own teeth on screen, exactly as we do
  • 3D scanning - comfortable digital impressions, no messy moulds
  • CAD/CAM technology - precisely designed crowns, bridges and restorations
  • Laser dentistry - minimally invasive treatment with quicker healing
